About Symbiotic Scholars (+/+)

Personalised learning designed for academic success.

Symbiotic Scholars (+/+) is more than a tutoring platform for me—it is a dream that has been deeply rooted in me since childhood. I’m Sheetal Mishra, and even as a five-year-old, I would watch my mother, a teacher, and find myself playing “teacher-teacher” with my soft toys and anyone around me. Somewhere along the way, teaching became more than a childhood game—it became a part of who I am. That is why Symbiotic Scholars is incredibly special and close to my heart. The name comes from mutualism, a biological relationship in which both organisms benefit. One beautiful example is mycorrhiza—the mutualistic relationship between plant roots and fungi. The fungi help the plant absorb water and minerals, while the plant provides nutrients to the fungi. Both give, both receive, and both grow.

That is exactly what we believe education should be. At Symbiotic Scholars, tutors aren’t here simply to complete a one-hour session or finish a syllabus. They are here to guide, listen, understand and grow with you. This is a safe space where you can talk about your day, share your thoughts, ask questions, make mistakes and simply be yourself. You don’t have to pretend. You’re allowed to make mistakes. And the beautiful part? We learn from our students too. They teach us empathy, emotions, new perspectives and skills we may not have mastered ourselves. In fact, this very website was created by our student Moksh Talwar, whose skills in coding taught us something new—and we couldn’t be prouder. Because at Symbiotic Scholars, we don't just teach. We grow together.
